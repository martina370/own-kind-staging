Clienteling is the practice of sales associates building personal, ongoing relationships with individual customers, using their preferences and history to make personalized recommendations. Read the full definition →

Who uses clienteling

Clienteling is most common in fashion, luxury retail, and boutique/specialty retail — anywhere a small number of repeat, high-value customers drive a disproportionate share of revenue, and where personal relationships genuinely influence purchase decisions.
