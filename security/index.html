ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certified
Own-Kind holds ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification (Cert No. 12034), the international standard for information security management. That means our approach to handling customer and client data — access control, risk management, incident response — has been independently audited against a recognised standard, not just self-reported.
Data protection
Own-Kind processes customer data under a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) in line with GDPR, and takes commercially reasonable steps to maintain compliance with GDPR and CCPA requirements. See our Data Processing Agreement and Privacy Policy for full detail.
