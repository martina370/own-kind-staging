Ruaraidh Grubb, Founder & CEO
From digital wardrobe to clienteling platform
Own-Kind began in 2018 as a digital wardrobe app — a way for people to catalogue their clothes, plan outfits and get styling advice, built to tackle a very real problem: UK shoppers were sitting on billions of pounds of unworn clothing, much of it destined for landfill.
The turning point came during COVID-19 lockdown. After connecting with Stella McCartney, Ruaraidh saw a different problem up close: with in-store capacity severely restricted, sales associates needed a way to keep serving clients personally, at a distance. He repurposed Own-Kind's existing technology to solve it — and the clienteling platform used by fashion and retail teams today was born.
Since then, Own-Kind has been built specifically for the teams who do this work every day: retail and fashion sales associates who succeed by knowing their clients, not by sending mass emails.
Our mission: help fashion and retail sales teams build real relationships with their customers — turning data into personal, timely outreach that feels like a trusted stylist, not a mass marketing blast.
