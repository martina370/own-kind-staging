Common questions
Why do I need Own-Kind if I already use Klaviyo?

Klaviyo is built for broadcast email and SMS marketing, sending the same campaign to a segment of customers. Own-Kind is built for 1:1 clienteling, where individual sales associates manage personal relationships with named clients, sending shoppable looks and outreach that feels personal rather than mass-marketed. Many Own-Kind clients use both together, and Own-Kind integrates directly with Klaviyo so customer data stays in sync between the two.

How is Own-Kind different from Salesforce or HubSpot?

Salesforce and HubSpot are general-purpose CRMs built to work for any industry, which means fashion and retail teams spend months configuring them to fit clienteling workflows. Own-Kind is purpose-built for fashion and retail sales teams out of the box, with client books, shoppable lookbooks, event and appointment tracking, and sales attribution designed specifically for how store associates and stylists actually work.
