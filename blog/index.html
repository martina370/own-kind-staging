Three things I've learned building technology for luxury retail: 1. Simplicity wins. If a stylist needs training to use your tool, it's too complex. 2. Data without action is just noise. Every insight must lead to a clear next step. 3. The best technology disappears. When a client receives a perfectly curated lookbook, they shouldn't think about the platform — they should think about how well their stylist knows them. We're relentlessly focused on these principles at Own-Kind, and it's driving results that exceed our expectations.
