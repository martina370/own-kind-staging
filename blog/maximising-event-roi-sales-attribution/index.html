Maximising event ROI with sales attribution

Luxury retail events — from intimate trunk shows to grand collection previews — are a cornerstone of client engagement. Yet for decades, measuring their true commercial impact has been one of the industry's most persistent challenges. How do you quantify the value of a champagne reception that leads to a purchase three weeks later?

The answer lies in sales attribution, and modern clienteling platforms are finally making it possible to draw a clear line from event invitation to eventual purchase. This capability is transforming how brands plan, execute, and evaluate their event strategies.

Traditional event measurement has relied on crude metrics: attendance numbers, immediate on-the-night sales, and anecdotal feedback. While these provide some indication of success, they miss the larger picture. Our data shows that 65% of event-influenced purchases occur in the two weeks following the event, not on the night itself. Without proper attribution, brands are dramatically undervaluing their event investments.

Modern attribution works by creating a digital thread that connects every touchpoint in the event journey. When a stylist sends an invitation through the platform, that interaction is logged. When the client RSVPs, attends, and subsequently purchases — whether in-store, online, or through a follow-up shoppable lookbook — the system attributes that revenue to the event.

This granular data enables far more sophisticated event planning. Brands can identify which event formats drive the highest ROI, which client segments respond best to different types of experiences, and which stylists are most effective at converting event attendees into purchasers. Armed with these insights, event budgets can be allocated with precision rather than intuition.

The most forward-thinking brands are also using attribution data to personalise the post-event experience. If a client showed particular interest in a specific collection at an event, their stylist can follow up with a targeted lookbook featuring those pieces. This seamless connection between event experience and personalised outreach is where the real magic happens.

Events will always be central to luxury retail — they create the emotional connections that drive brand loyalty. But in 2025 and beyond, the brands that thrive will be those that can prove the value of every event, optimise their strategies based on data, and create seamless journeys from invitation to purchase.
